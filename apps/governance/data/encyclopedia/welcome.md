# Welcome to the Encyclopedia

This is the society's encyclopedia - a collaborative knowledge base for documenting procedures, policies, and institutional knowledge.

## What Goes Here?

The encyclopedia documents concepts, institutions, and practices relevant to the society. Each article follows a consistent structure:

1. **What It Is**: General definition and explanation
2. **History**: Historical context and precedents
3. **In the Benjamin Franklin Society**: Specific implementation in our society

Topics include:
- **Governance concepts**: [[Sortition]], motion processes, deliberative procedures
- **Organizational structures**: [[College]]s, services, associations
- **Services**: Documentation for each service domain
- **History**: Founding stories, major decisions, milestones
- **How-Tos**: Guides for platform usage and civic participation

## Getting Started

To create a new article, add a markdown file to `data/encyclopedia/` with frontmatter metadata.

**Article structure:**
```markdown
---
title: Your Topic
category: Governance
author: Your Name
created: 2026-05-25
---

# Your Topic

## What It Is

General definition and explanation of the concept...

## History

Historical context, precedents, and evolution...

## In the Benjamin Franklin Society

How we specifically implement or use this...
```

## Wiki Links

You can link between articles using double brackets: `[[article-name]]`

For example: [[Sortition]] or [[College]]
