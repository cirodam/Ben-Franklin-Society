I recognize the enormity of the crises that beset my nation and all humankind, and so I vow the following:
I shall hold the dignity of every human being to be equal, sacred, and inviolable.
I shall bear witness to want, injustice, and suffering.
I shall hold my surplus as belonging to the common good.
I shall discharge my obligations with dispatch.
I shall hold my charges as a sacred trust.
I shall acquit myself of whatever burden falls to me.
This is my word, freely given, among those who have given theirs.
