# The Ben Franklin Society
## Of [County Name]

### Foundational Document on the Social Insurance Fund
*Article VI of the Bylaws*

---

## Preamble

The Social Insurance Fund is the Ben Franklin Society's material commitment to every member who can no longer fully participate in the community's economic life. It exists because the Society holds that human presence generates value — that the elderly member who spent decades contributing, the disabled member whose capacity is diminished through no fault of their own, are full members of the community whose dignity and basic security are the community's responsibility. The Fund is not charity. It is the community keeping its word.

---

## Article I — Nature and Purpose

**§1. Purpose.** The Social Insurance Fund provides regular monthly payments in Franks to members who have reached the community's established retirement age and to members whose capacity to contribute is diminished by disability. These payments are a right of membership, not a benefit conferred at discretion. The Fund exists to ensure that no member of the Society faces material insecurity in old age or disability.

**§2. Not Charity.** Payments from the Fund are not charity and shall not be administered or received as such. They are the community's fulfillment of a mutual obligation — the reciprocal commitment that makes membership meaningful. A member receiving Fund payments has not become a burden. They have reached the condition for which the Fund exists.

**§3. Dual Paths.** The Fund administers two distinct payment paths: the Retirement Path for members who have reached the established retirement age, and the Disability Path for members whose contribution capacity is diminished by disability. Both paths provide the same monthly payment. They are tracked separately for purposes of accountability, reporting, and Assembly oversight.

**§4. Faithful Administration.** The Fund administers payments in accordance with the terms established by the Assembly. It does not exercise discretion over eligibility beyond applying the Assembly's established criteria, nor over payment amounts beyond disbursing what the Assembly has determined. Policy belongs to the community. Administration belongs to the Fund.

---

## Article II — The Retirement Path

**§5. Eligibility.** Any member who has reached the retirement age established by the Assembly is entitled to monthly payments from the Fund through the Retirement Path. Eligibility is automatic upon reaching retirement age. No application, means test, or demonstration of need is required.

**§6. Retirement Age.** The retirement age is established by the Assembly and reviewed periodically. In establishing and reviewing the retirement age the Assembly shall consider the health and life expectancy of the membership, the capacity of the Fund, and the community's values regarding the dignity of aging. *[Placeholder: retirement age to be established by Assembly resolution.]*

**§7. Continuation.** Retirement Path payments continue for the life of the member. They do not lapse, expire, or require renewal. A member who has entered the Retirement Path remains in it.

**§8. Dues.** Retirement Path members continue to pay dues as members in good standing. The monthly Fund payment and the dues obligation are separate — the payment is the community's commitment to the member, the dues are the member's ongoing contribution to the community. Both continue.

## Article III — The Disability Path

**§9. Eligibility.** Any member whose capacity to contribute to the community's economic life is diminished by disability is eligible for monthly payments from the Fund through the Disability Path. Disability is assessed on a spectrum of contribution capacity rather than as a binary condition. Assessment is conducted jointly by the Medical College and the Caregivers College in accordance with criteria established by the Assembly.

**§10. Definition of Disability.** The Assembly shall establish the community's definition of disability for purposes of Fund eligibility, with input from the Medical College and the Caregivers College. The definition shall be reviewed periodically. *[Placeholder: disability definition and assessment criteria to be established by Assembly resolution with College input.]*

**§11. Assessment.** Disability assessment is conducted jointly by the Medical College and the Caregivers College. Assessment shall be conducted with dignity and good faith. The presumption is that a member seeking Disability Path support has genuine need. *[Placeholder: assessment process and procedures to be established in standing rules.]*

**§12. Reassessment.** Disability conditions change over time. Disability Path members may be subject to periodic reassessment. Reassessment shall be conducted with the same dignity and good faith as initial assessment. A member whose circumstances have improved may transition off the Disability Path. A member whose circumstances have worsened remains fully supported. *[Placeholder: reassessment schedule and process to be established in standing rules.]*

**§13. Dues.** Disability Path members continue to pay dues as members in good standing, subject to such hardship provisions as the Assembly establishes. The Fund payment and the dues obligation are separate obligations of the mutual relationship between member and community.

---

## Article IV — Payments

**§14. Monthly Payment.** The Fund issues a monthly payment in Franks to all eligible members on both the Retirement Path and the Disability Path. The payment amount is the same for all eligible members regardless of path, circumstances, or prior contribution history. The community's commitment is equal.

**§15. Payment Amount.** The monthly payment amount is established by the Assembly and reviewed annually. In establishing the payment amount the Assembly shall consider the cost of basic dignified living within the community, the capacity of the Fund, and the community's values. *[Placeholder: monthly payment amount to be established by Assembly resolution.]*

**§16. Delivery.** Payments are delivered by direct credit to the member's Community Bank account on a regular monthly schedule. The Fund shall coordinate with the Community Bank and Treasury to ensure payments are made accurately and on time without interruption.

**§17. Interruption.** Fund payments shall not be interrupted, delayed, or withheld except in circumstances of genuine Fund insolvency addressed under Article V. Administrative difficulties, ledger disruptions, or institutional failures shall not delay payments to members who depend upon them. The Fund shall maintain operational protocols to ensure payment continuity under adverse conditions.

---

## Article V — Funding and Reserves

**§18. Funding.** The Social Insurance Fund is funded through membership dues. A portion of dues collected by the Treasury is appropriated by the Assembly to the Fund annually. The Fund is not a pass-through — it holds reserves sufficient to sustain payments through periods of reduced dues revenue or unexpected increases in eligible membership.

**§19. Reserve Requirement.** The Fund shall maintain a minimum reserve established by the Assembly. The reserve shall be sufficient to sustain monthly payments to all current eligible members for a period determined by the Assembly without additional appropriation. This buffer ensures that short-term disruptions to dues revenue do not interrupt payments. *[Placeholder: minimum reserve period to be established by Assembly resolution.]*

**§20. Reserve Management.** The Fund shall hold its reserves in Franks in its Community Bank account. Reserves shall not be invested, lent, or otherwise deployed in ways that place them at risk. The Fund's obligation to its members is immediate and certain; its reserves must be equally certain.

**§21. Annual Review.** The Assembly shall review the Fund's financial condition annually, including total obligations, current reserves, reserve adequacy relative to the minimum requirement, and projected future obligations based on membership demographics. The Assembly shall adjust appropriations as necessary to maintain reserve adequacy.

**§22. Reserve Insufficiency.** If the Fund's reserves fall below the minimum requirement, the Fund shall immediately notify the Assembly. The Assembly shall treat reserve insufficiency as a matter of urgent priority and shall act to restore reserves through additional appropriation, adjustment of payment amounts, or other measures as the community determines. The community shall not allow the Fund to reach insolvency without deliberate decision.

## Article VI — Oversight and Transparency

**§23. Oversight.** The Social Insurance Fund is overseen by the Assembly with input from the Medical College and Caregivers College on matters of eligibility assessment and disability criteria. The Assembly retains authority over all material determinations — retirement age, disability definition, payment amount, reserve requirement.

**§24. Transparency.** The Fund's financial condition — total reserves, total obligations, number of members on each path, payment amounts, and appropriations received — shall be open to inspection by any member at any time. The Fund's operations are the community's business.

**§25. Annual Report.** The Fund shall publish an annual report covering its financial condition, the number of members served on each path, payment totals, reserve status, and any material changes in eligibility or payment amounts during the year. The report shall be presented to the Assembly and made available to all members.

**§26. Member Privacy.** While the Fund's aggregate financial condition is fully transparent, the individual eligibility status of specific members — particularly those on the Disability Path — is private. The Fund shall not disclose which individual members are receiving payments without the member's consent, except as required by the Assembly's oversight function.
